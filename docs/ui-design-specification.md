# UI Design Specification

Palette:
- Primary Navy `#071D49`
- Electric Blue `#0866F5`
- Blue Accent `#2F80FF`
- Background `#F6F8FC`
- Surface `#FFFFFF`
- Primary Text `#102347`
- Secondary Text `#71809B`
- Border `#E7EDF5`
- Success `#16A765`
- Warning `#F5A524`
- Danger `#E5484D`

Open Sans is the primary font. Spacing follows a 4 px scale. Cards use 14 px radius. Buttons use 12 px radius and 48 px touch targets.

Implemented Fase 1 components: `AppButton`, `AppTextField`, `AppCard`, `AppSearchField`, `AppBadge`, and `AppEmptyState`.

Planned components: bottom sheet, dialog, dropdown, loading, error, product card, transaction tile, price display, quantity selector, payment card, bottom navigation, and confirmation dialog.
